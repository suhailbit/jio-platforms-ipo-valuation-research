# Jio Platforms — IPO Valuation & Fundamental Equity Research

An Excel-based equity research portfolio project examining Jio Platforms' IPO pricing, business fundamentals, valuation relative to Bharti Airtel, and illustrative post-listing valuation scenarios.

**Market:** India | **Currency:** INR (crore unless stated)

> **Important:** This is educational portfolio research, not a current investment recommendation. The accompanying report describes an IPO and listing dated October 2026, using press-reported figures and derived estimates that have **not been independently checked against primary filings or exchange records**. Verify the listing, prices, financial data and assumptions before presenting them as confirmed facts.

## Research question

How did Jio Platforms' IPO valuation compare with its business fundamentals and Bharti Airtel, and what do alternative growth and valuation assumptions imply for its share value?

## Research approach

1. **IPO structure and pricing:** Reviewed issue size, pricing, subscription, listing premium, proceeds and ownership.
2. **Business fundamentals:** Examined revenue, EBITDA, net profit, margins, subscribers, ARPU and potential growth drivers.
3. **Relative valuation:** Compared Jio with Bharti Airtel using P/E, EV/EBITDA, EV/sales, margin and leverage metrics.
4. **Scenario valuation:** Applied illustrative FY28E EBITDA growth rates and EV/EBITDA multiples to derive bear, base and bull values.
5. **Risk analysis:** Considered tariff increases, spectrum obligations, debt-like liabilities, competition, capital intensity and promoter control.

The report additionally estimates that treating ₹1.1 lakh crore of deferred spectrum payments as debt-like, if not already included, would lower scenario values by approximately ₹119 per share.

## Research conclusion

The report's assessment is that the IPO was reasonably valued relative to Airtel, but the subsequent price increase reduced the apparent margin of safety. Its base-case illustration is below the 20 November snapshot price, with the valuation sensitive to tariff changes, earnings growth, spectrum liabilities and the multiple investors are willing to pay.

## Limitations and next steps

- Verify the reported IPO, listing, market price and financial figures against exchange and company filings.
- Reconcile revenue definitions and reported debt, leases and deferred spectrum obligations.
- Avoid treating one annualised quarter as a dependable full-year forecast.
- Add a discounted cash flow model, cash-flow and capital-expenditure analysis, and more telecom peers.
- Validate scenario assumptions and include historical operating trends.
